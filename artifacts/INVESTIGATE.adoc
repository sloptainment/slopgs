= INVESTIGATE — open questions and engine gaps

Everything here is unresolved: driver behaviour not yet recovered, engine
behaviour that knowingly differs from the driver, and fitted (`[F]`) values
standing in for either. Settled facts live in `src/unit.c` as assertions.

*Do not read this file whole.* `grep -n '^== ' artifacts/INVESTIGATE.adoc`
lists every entry, one line each, prefixed with its id. Jump to one with
`grep -n '\[#i-event-times\]' artifacts/INVESTIGATE.adoc` and Read about
40 lines from there. Source comments and `unit.c` TODO reasons cite these
ids (`INVESTIGATE.adoc#i-event-times`).

Every entry has the same four fields: *Known* (what is settled, with VMAs),
*Open* (what is not), *Settle by* (the observation that would close it),
*Where* (code and tests). Delete an entry once it is closed; the closing
assertion in `unit.c` is the record.

Corpus baseline: *MEAN r 0.998, residual −55.31 dB*.

[#i-envelope-cadence]
== i-envelope-cadence: top-up runs on a 2048-frame tick, the driver's once per 1024-frame Mix ([F])

Known:: `0x13054` drains due events, advances every voice once and promotes
controllers once per call. FillBuffer always hands it 1024 frames
(`0x181fa`; unit.c "a service call (ControlLogic::Mix) is 1024 frames"),
so top-up, which runs once per call (`0x12be7`), runs every 1024 frames.
Probes 20/21 need a top-up period ≥ 1024 frames (512 leaves 43 of 48
voices). `0x12bd6` passes top-up the call's end time (`0x12bdf`), so its
fast releases may start there, not at the call's start. `0x12860` derives
per-rate constants never traced: `device+0x10` = rate/100 (220),
`device+0x188` = rate·40/1000 (882), `device+0x1a0` = 20 at 22050.
A/B, `TOPUP_INTERVAL_FRAMES` 2048 (shipped) → 1024 (the driver's): mean
−55.31 → −55.29 dB; `21_steal_policy` −49.24 → −48.58, `CrystalOscillator`
−40.16 → −39.94, `10_polyphony` −25.05 → −25.27.
Open:: Whether top-up's releases start at the call's end, which the engine
does not model and may be what 1024 is missing; what the `0x12860`
constants drive.
Settle by:: Trace `0x12b6a`'s use of its time argument, model it, re-run
the A/B and replace the S5.4 TODO.
Where:: `voice.c` `TOPUP_INTERVAL_FRAMES`, `voice_topup_tick`; unit.c TODOs
`S5.4 top-up cadence value`, `S3.4.1/S3.4.2 envelope update cadence`.

[#i-eg1-bounds]
== i-eg1-bounds: EG1 bounds a segment only at its attack and decay end

Known:: A segment ends at min(cap, LFO, EG1, EG2), clipped to the Mix call
(unit.c). EG2 is read at its attack end and at a decay's or release's
quarter, half and end (`0x18a7a`). The engine's EG1 is per-sample
geometric and bounds a segment only at its attack and decay end; in
sustain and release it bounds nothing, so a release runs to the LFO bound
or the cap. An EG's first bound is its attack (`0x198b6`), so a zero attack
makes a zero-length first segment that primes the mixer at the note's time
(unit.c, for EG2). The engine leaves the previous-segment sums that size
the first stair at 0 (`0x18f23`).
Open:: Whether EG1 (`0x18d28`) uses `0x18a7a`'s reads; whether the priming
segment also sets the previous-segment sums. Removing the release floor
cost `Kot_and_A64-GENERAL_SERUM` 0.91 dB, `serum_opening` 0.55 and
`HueArme-Weekend` 0.54 (mean −50.01 → −55.20); against the build before
segments were per voice, `41_sustain_decay_curve` is still behind
(−63.09 → −61.33).
Settle by:: Disassemble `0x18d28`'s bound path; gdb the oracle on a
zero-attack voice's first two segments.
Where:: `voice.c` `env_base_frames_to_change`, `voice_segment_frames`;
`render.c` `starting`.

[#i-event-times]
== i-event-times: which dispatched events act at their own time

Known:: RecordMIDI queues every event with its time; at Mix start
`0x12bd6` pops each note event with its time (`0x16cac`) and calls the
note-off path `0x19a2c` with it (`0x12c53`, `0x12cb9`, `0x12cf5`,
`0x12da5`), which releases the EGs at max(time, start + 1) (`0x19a3d`;
unit.c "A note-off releases at its own time"). ScheduleFastRelease
(`0x19aa4`) uses the event's time for a voice still held, or the pending
note-off's (`+0x128`) for one already released. Live controllers are
queued and folded at the call's end (`0x16b46`, unit.c). The engine times
note-off, CC120, CC123, the Mono release, CC64's lift and a note-on's
retrigger and key-group choke this way.
Open:: Which call site is which event; whether CC64's lift releases at its
own time; the engine's queue holds 64 nodes per controller, the driver's limit
(`0x16bae`'s allocation) unknown.
Settle by:: Map the four `0x19a2c` call sites to their events; trace CC64
from `0x131c0` to its consumers.
Where:: `voice.c` `release_at_event`, `fast_release_at_event`; `synth.c`
`ctl_record`, `CTL_QUEUE`.

[#i-vibrato-depth]
== i-vibrato-depth: the reference's vibrato is ~5% shallower than the render

Known:: `13_edge` 16.6–17.6 s (Piano 1, CC1=127, authored 47+1 c at 5.2 Hz):
a sinusoid fit to the 8th harmonic's track (1024-point STFT, same tracker
for both) gives reference 44.1 c at 5.215 Hz and render 46.2 c at 5.185 Hz.
Phase relative to each note's own onset differs by 28°, which is within
the onset uncertainty. This segment is the whole `13_edge` change (−38.73 dB before
`edf5b31`, −38.55 now).
Open:: Where the ~2 c goes. The engine now reads the LFO once per segment
at its end, as the driver does (`0x195ba`; ≤ 530 frames here), which a
sinusoid sampled at 1/8 cycle loses 2–5% of its fundamental to; `13_edge`
against the oracle went −46.07 → −54.47 dB with it. Not re-measured
against the reference.
Settle by:: Probe 51's reference (`probe-results/51.flac`): Shakuhachi's
18 c inherent vibrato and Tremolo Str's 10 Hz tremolo at CC1=0, measured
the same way.
Where:: `voice.c` `voice_lfo`, `lfo_frames_to_change`; no test.

[#i-bankprog-lookahead]
== i-bankprog-lookahead: Bank/Program look-ahead within a tick ([F])

Known:: At one tick a note takes the last Program Change before the next
note event of that tick (probe 40 13/13, probe 38 7/8). The driver's getter
(`0x16c50`) adopts every pending value with key ≤ the query time, which
does not predict this. Notes are set up at Mix start, once the whole
call's events are queued (`0x12bd6` pops each note with its time,
`0x16cac`), and read a controller at the note's own time (`0x12dc3`), so a
same-time value after the note in file order is adopted; unit.c asserts
it for pitch bend. Whether Program is one of those queues is unverified.
Open:: The mechanism; probe 38 case F contradicts probe 40 case G on
identical input.
Settle by:: Re-capture both cases twice.
Where:: `smf.c` `SMF_BANKPROG_LOOKAHEAD`; no test.

[#i-part-model]
== i-part-model: part state is per channel, not per Part

Known:: The driver keeps tuning, rhythm flag and RPN state per Part and fans
each channel message out to every Part whose RCV CHANNEL matches (`0x13267`);
the per-part SysEx handlers index by the address nibble (`0x13797`,
`0x137ae`). The engine keeps state per channel and maps the nibble through
RCV CHANNEL once. Equivalent while the map is a permutation.
Open:: Two Parts on one channel (doubled notes) and a flag following a later
RCV CHANNEL change are not modeled.
Settle by:: A probe that maps two Parts to one channel.
Where:: `synth.c` `part_to_channel`; no test.

[#i-kmixer]
== i-kmixer: 44.1 kHz references pass through kmixer's resampler

Known:: The driver outputs 22050 Hz only (`0x1a710`). kmixer's 22050→44100
stage is a 60-tap-per-phase float32 FIR, two phases, accumulated at x87
default precision and truncated at the store (`kmixer 0x2c0a0`, `0x2c840`).
Zero-order hold and linear interpolation are excluded (0.23%/0.87% match).
Open:: The tap values (filled at runtime, not static) and the rate gate.
Settle by:: A kernel-debugger dump of the tap buffer at `0x2c0a0`.
Where:: `src/vsti/vsti.c` models it; the engine does not.

[#i-rate-parity]
== i-rate-parity: 6 items fail rate_parity.py from aliasing ×1 has and ×2 does not

Known:: The engine is rate-exact: every ×2 frame 2n equals ×1 frame n bit for
bit on all 71 corpus MIDIs (silent tails aside: the CLI's 4096-frame stop
check is 2048 ×1 frames at ×2). A segment ends at a Mix call's end, so
`smf.c` renders a song's tail on the block grid too. Phase is in `PHASE_ONE` units, and event
times, the bend ramp, EG1 and the amplitude ramp all step on the
`BASE_RATE` grid. `rate_parity.py` (soxr) still fails these:
+

|02_keyrange |0.9707 |0.00 |0.02
|30_tune_clamp_bend |0.9122 |0.01 |0.67
|31_tune_clamp_bend_sine |0.8176 |0.00 |0.00
|radio |0.9313 |1170.86 |6.77
|wild-sweep |0.9891 |0.02 |0.06
|Strobe-faffaeefafaefae |0.9929 |0.05 |0.09

+
Each is content above 11025 Hz: ×1 folds it into the band, ×2 renders it
unfolded and soxr removes it (31 goes silent at key 127, 14.2 s).
Open:: Whether `rate_parity.py` should compare by 2:1 decimation, which
keeps ×1's aliasing and passes all 70 exactly, or keep soxr and exempt these.

Where:: `artifacts/rate_parity.py` `render()`.
