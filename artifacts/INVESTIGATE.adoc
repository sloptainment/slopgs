= INVESTIGATE — open questions and engine gaps

Everything here is unresolved: driver behaviour not yet recovered, engine
behaviour that knowingly differs from the driver, and fitted (`[F]`) values
standing in for either. Settled facts live in `src/unit.c` as assertions.

*Do not read this file whole.* `grep -n '^== ' artifacts/INVESTIGATE.adoc`
lists every entry, one line each, prefixed with its id. Jump to one with
`grep -n '\[#i-release-floor\]' artifacts/INVESTIGATE.adoc` and Read about
40 lines from there. Source comments and `unit.c` TODO reasons cite these
ids (`INVESTIGATE.adoc#i-release-floor`).

Every entry has the same four fields: *Known* (what is settled, with VMAs),
*Open* (what is not), *Settle by* (the observation that would close it),
*Where* (code and tests). Delete an entry once it is closed; the closing
assertion in `unit.c` is the record.

Corpus baseline: *MEAN r 0.932, residual −32.98 dB*.

[#i-ceiling-populations]
== i-ceiling-populations: the per-voice gain ceiling splits the corpus (audible, 13.9 dB on one item)

Known:: The ceiling (`TABLE[0]` = 4095/8192 of `0x1bfc0`, `0x18e09`) and the
`+1200` at `0x19bcd` are both in the driver. With them, `41_sustain_decay_curve`
sits at −37.1 dB; without the clamp it would be −51.1. Exactly its 3 notes with
region attenuation 0 clamp. Raising the ceiling 0.75 dB recovers `41` but
costs `27_gain_curves` 8.9 dB and `45_release_from_level` 15.8 dB, whose
references (Sine `008:080`, region −314 hdB, velocity/CC7 127) do flat-top.
Probe 50: slopgs's flat top sits 1.61 dB high, sub-knee levels 1.35–2.96 dB
low. The two populations disagree by 0.7–1.1 dB, so no uniform level fixes
both.
Open:: Which term of the attenuation sum differs: region/wsmp attenuation
(0 vs −314) or the velocity-table lookup at 100 vs 127.
Settle by:: Term-by-term comparison of the engine's sum against
`0x19b94`–`0x19c2a` on probe 50's sections.
Where:: `voice.c` `voice_update_gain`, `atten_to_gain`; no test.

[#i-release-floor]
== i-release-floor: RELEASE_FLOOR_S has no counterpart in the driver ([F], audible)

Known:: `0x197dc` sets `release = release * L / 1000` and nothing else
(`0x19801`, `0x19815`). Removing the 60 ms floor: mean −32.48 → −32.28 dB;
`37_rac_volume_order` −30.7 → −44.6, `sine-gate`/`sine2` ~0.8 dB better;
`25_pan_law` +5.8, `27_gain_curves` +5.4, `45`/`46`/`40` +3.4–3.8,
`serum_opening` +1.5 dB worse.
Open:: What the floor compensates for. Short releases render as one linear
segment in the driver (see <<i-segment-length>>); the engine's release is
per-sample geometric.
Settle by:: Implement segment-shaped release and re-run the A/B without the
floor.
Where:: `voice.c` `RELEASE_FLOOR_S`; unit.c TODO `S5.6 a 10 ms authored release`.

[#i-segment-length]
== i-segment-length: the engine's segment is a fixed 256 frames, the driver's is per voice ([F])

Known:: `0x19644` (per voice, from `0x130d3`) renders in segments. At
`0x196cd` the segment end is min(now + `+0x108`, buffer end); `0x196f0`
resets `+0x108` to the cap `[+0x104]+0x18` = `(rate+19)/20` = 1103 frames
(`0x12901`); then pitch (`0x195a2`, called at `0x1970c`) and volume
(`0x19490`) are evaluated at the segment end, and each lowers `+0x108` to
its sources' next change: EG1 and the LFO on the volume side (`0x194cc`,
`0x1950a`), the LFO and EG2 on the pitch side (`0x195dd`, `0x19619`). At
note-on `+0x108` starts at the LFO bound (`0x19c56`) and the EGs lower it
(`0x19c7a`–`0x19ce3`). The LFO bound (`0x1891e`) is the remaining delay,
then `0x20000/step` frames (1/8 cycle); an LFO with all four depths 0
reports 44100 (`0x188f0`). Every gm.dls patch seen so far carries CC1→pitch
47 c, so its segment is ≤ 459 frames at 6 Hz (Sine, Square) and 530 at
5.2 Hz (Piano); probes 45/46 measure ~512 and probe 44 implies 24–28 ms.
The mixer ramps gain linearly in amplitude to the value at the segment end
(`0x18fba`). The buffer is the caller's KS buffer (`0x181fa`).
Open:: Whether pitch ramps or holds across a segment (bears on
<<i-vibrato-depth>>). Whether the per-voice minimum also fixes the
decay-dominated items: a fixed 512 cost `08_reverb` +9.5 and `03_velocity`
+7.1 dB (sweep at the old −31 dB baseline: 128 −31.18, 256 −31.14,
512 −30.90; re-run against −32.98).
Settle by:: Replace the unit.c TODO first (its reason, "segment length is
the caller's KS buffer, not a driver constant", predates this): assert the
LFO bound `0x20000/step` and 44100 at zero depth, and the segment as the
minimum above. Then model segments per voice in place of
`GAIN_SEGMENT_FRAMES` and gate on the corpus, watching `08_reverb`,
`03_velocity`, `13_edge` and probes 44–46.
Where:: `render.c` `GAIN_SEGMENT_FRAMES`; `voice.h` `LFO_UPDATE_FRAMES`,
`SERVICE_BLOCK_FRAMES` (also smf.c's event split); unit.c TODO `S6.6
ramp/envelope update cadence value`.

[#i-envelope-cadence]
== i-envelope-cadence: envelopes and top-up run per sample / per tick, not per service call ([F])

Known:: `0x13054` drains due events, advances every voice once and promotes
controllers once per call; the frame count comes from the KS descriptor
(`0x181da`). Top-up runs once per dispatcher call (`0x12be7`). Probes 20/21
need a top-up period ≥ 1024 frames (512 leaves 43 of 48 voices). Note-off
lattice 0–27 ms (probe 46). `0x12860` derives per-rate constants never
traced: `device+0x10` = rate/100 (220), `device+0x188` = rate·40/1000 (882),
`device+0x1a0` = 20 at 22050.
Open:: The EG state machine's own cadence and what feeds the per-call ramp
steps; what the `0x12860` constants drive.
Settle by:: Trace readers of `device+0x10`, `+0x188`, `+0x1a0`; trace
`0x19644` and callees.
Where:: `voice.c` `TOPUP_INTERVAL_FRAMES`, `voice_step_envelope`; unit.c TODOs
`S5.4 top-up cadence value`, `S3.4.1/S3.4.2 envelope update cadence`.

[#i-bend-ramp]
== i-bend-ramp: pitch-bend ramp duration 320 frames vs measured 448–512 ([F])

Known:: A bend glides linearly over a fixed duration independent of step
size (probe 33, 10–90% 20–24 ms for 200–4000 c). 320 frames is the optimum on
`33_ramp` (−34.04 dB); per-rep edge timing reads 448–512. The reference's
bend timing clusters on a 512-frame grid and is bimodal (4.2 and 16.5 ms).
`town.mid` rises and falls glide at different rates (~2.2 vs 4.2 c/ms).
Open:: The duration, the bimodality, the asymmetry.
Settle by:: Re-derive both readings; `32.flac`'s alignment drifts 422 ms
non-linearly, so do not fit against it.
Where:: `voice.c` `RAMP_HORIZON_FRAMES`, `ramp_reaim`; no test.

[#i-vibrato-depth]
== i-vibrato-depth: the reference's vibrato is ~5% shallower than the render

Known:: `13_edge` 16.6–17.6 s (Piano 1, CC1=127, authored 47+1 c at 5.2 Hz):
a sinusoid fit to the 8th harmonic's track (1024-point STFT, same tracker
for both) gives reference 44.1 c at 5.215 Hz and render 46.2 c at 5.185 Hz.
Phase relative to each note's own onset differs by 28°, which is within
the onset uncertainty. This segment is the whole `13_edge` change (−38.73 dB before
`edf5b31`, −38.55 now).
Open:: Where the ~2 c goes. Suspect: the driver samples the LFO once per
segment (≤ 530 frames here, <<i-segment-length>>) where the engine samples
every 64; a held or ramped sinusoid sampled at 1/8 cycle loses 2–5% of its
fundamental.
Settle by:: Probe 51's reference (`probe-results/51.flac`): Shakuhachi's
18 c inherent vibrato and Tremolo Str's 10 Hz tremolo at CC1=0, measured
the same way.
Where:: `voice.c` `voice_lfo`, `voices_advance_lfo`; `voice.h`
`LFO_UPDATE_FRAMES`; no test.

[#i-bankprog-lookahead]

Settle by:: Re-capture both cases twice.
Where:: `smf.c` `SMF_BANKPROG_LOOKAHEAD`; no test.

[#i-part-model]
== i-part-model: part state is per channel, not per Part

Known:: The driver keeps tuning, rhythm flag and RPN state per Part and fans
each channel message out to every Part whose RCV CHANNEL matches (`0x13267`);
the per-part SysEx handlers index by the address nibble (`0x13797`,
`0x137ae`). The engine keeps state per channel and maps the nibble through
RCV CHANNEL once. Equivalent while the map is a permutation.
Open:: Two Parts on one channel (doubled notes) and a flag following a later
RCV CHANNEL change are not modeled.
Settle by:: A probe that maps two Parts to one channel.
Where:: `synth.c` `part_to_channel`; no test.

[#i-kmixer]
== i-kmixer: 44.1 kHz references pass through kmixer's resampler

Known:: The driver outputs 22050 Hz only (`0x1a710`). kmixer's 22050→44100
stage is a 60-tap-per-phase float32 FIR, two phases, accumulated at x87
default precision and truncated at the store (`kmixer 0x2c0a0`, `0x2c840`).
Zero-order hold and linear interpolation are excluded (0.23%/0.87% match).
Open:: The tap values (filled at runtime, not static) and the rate gate.
Settle by:: A kernel-debugger dump of the tap buffer at `0x2c0a0`.
Where:: `src/vsti/vsti.c` models it; the engine does not.

[#i-rate-parity]
== i-rate-parity: 6 items fail rate_parity.py from aliasing ×1 has and ×2 does not

Known:: The engine is rate-exact: every ×2 frame 2n equals ×1 frame n bit for
bit on all 70 corpus MIDIs (silent tails aside: the CLI's 4096-frame stop
check is 2048 ×1 frames at ×2). Phase is in `PHASE_ONE` units, and event
times, the bend ramp, EG1 and the amplitude ramp all step on the
`BASE_RATE` grid. `rate_parity.py` (soxr) still fails these:
+
[%autowidth]
|===
|item |env_r |pitch_c |band_db
|02_keyrange |0.9714 |0.00 |0.02
|30_tune_clamp_bend |0.9054 |0.02 |0.71
|31_tune_clamp_bend_sine |0.8189 |0.00 |0.00
|radio |0.9264 |1173.09 |6.44
|wild-sweep |0.9879 |0.03 |0.07
|Strobe-faffaeefafaefae |0.9928 |0.06 |0.09
|===
+
Each is content above 11025 Hz: ×1 folds it into the band, ×2 renders it
unfolded and soxr removes it (31 goes silent at key 127, 14.2 s).
Open:: Whether `rate_parity.py` should compare by 2:1 decimation, which
keeps ×1's aliasing and passes all 70 exactly, or keep soxr and exempt these.
Settle by:: A policy decision; no measurement is outstanding.
Where:: `artifacts/rate_parity.py` `render()`.

[#i-cosmetic]
== i-cosmetic: driver unknowns with no output consequence for gm.dls

Each is unrecovered; none changes what slopgs renders today.

* `region+0x28 > 0` is a hard region-selection gate (`0x1473e`); its meaning
  is unknown (candidate: wave data ready). Matters for partly-resolved
  collections.
* Who copies the wave rate into `region+0x14` (`0x18f6f`); `0x1456e` has no
  caller.
* Where wave `fmt`/`data`/`wsmp` are finally decoded after the ptbl fixup;
  and the region/wave wsmp merge when a region has no wsmp.
* Whether a loop end past the decoded data is clamped.
* `device+0xf10` (RPN select) at device-add: nothing writes it.
* Any velocity-side reader of the linear table `0x1bfd4` (pan reads it).
* Where promoted CC7/CC11 values become gain (the table use is asserted).
* `articBlock+0x38`'s identity; the device object at `voice+0x104` beyond
  `+0x0/+0x4/+0x8/+0xc/+0x18`; the `+0x118` timestamp pair.
* Purpose of the `edit` chunk (5 sites) and of ptbl placeholder `+0x2f`;
  `0x1469e`'s field for a wave object.
* Why the pool splits 48+6 rather than 54 flat.
* Whether the scalar mixer family matches MMX bit for bit (unreached).
* Whether output spectrum varies with velocity (code says no).
* Probe 18 measured a 70 ms choke; the bound is 315 samples = 14.29 ms
  (`0x19834`, rate 22050 from `0x12b40`). Re-measure on an isolated choke.
